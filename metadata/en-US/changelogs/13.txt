## New

* Subtracks 3.0.0 is a ground-up rewrite in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with a fresh interface
* New Home tab with recently played, most played, recently added and rediscover rows, plus genre and decade shortcuts
* Sortable and filterable album, artist and playlist views, with starred-only filters
* Full library search across albums, artists, playlists and songs
* Offline downloads with per-album, artist and playlist progress, download quality and a metered-connection toggle
* An editable queue: play next, add to queue, reorder, remove and jump to any track
* Adaptive theming that draws its colours from the current artwork
* Multiple servers, scrobbling, per-network stream quality and long-press context menus
